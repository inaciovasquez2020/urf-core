Corrected abstract (2026-11-26)
Bounded per-output AC0 circuit fan-in and arity do not bound aggregate information per parallel update. The historical unrestricted normalization and universal EntropyDepth separation are not established. We give an exact n-1-round theorem for seeded path broadcast under explicit synchronous nearest-neighbor and distributed-output hypotheses. It is not a SAT decision lower bound. TRAP remains unproved.
