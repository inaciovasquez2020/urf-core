\begin{conjecture}[Flip preserves horizontality]
If $T \subset J^k(X)$ supports a horizontal normal function, then
$\mathsf{Flip}(T)$ also supports a horizontal normal function.
\end{conjecture}

\noindent
BOUNDARY: $\mathbb{Q}$-linearity of the Gauss--Manin connection does not by
itself prove that the rational Hodge envelope defining
$\mathsf{Flip}(T)$ supports a horizontal normal function.  A separate
preservation theorem for that envelope is required.
