\begin{conjecture}[Mixed Hodge Horizontal Rigidity]
Let $T \subset J^k(X)$ be a real subtorus supporting a horizontal normal function,
possibly in a mixed Hodge variation.
Then
\[
T = \mathsf{Flip}(T),
\]
and hence $T$ is defined over $\mathbb{Q}$.
\end{conjecture}

\noindent
BOUNDARY: horizontality of $\mathsf{Flip}(T)$ does not by itself prove
$T=\mathsf{Flip}(T)$.  A separate rigidity theorem identifying the real
subtorus with its rational Hodge envelope is required.
