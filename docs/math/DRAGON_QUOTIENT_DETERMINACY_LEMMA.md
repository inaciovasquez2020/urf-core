# DraG0n Quotient Determinacy Lemma

## Status

CONDITIONAL

## Statement

Fix a diagnostic object `D` and augmentation budget `k`.

Assume the Explicit Admissibility Axioms.

Then every equivalence class in
\[
\mathcal M_{\mathrm{aug}}^{(k)}(D)/{\equiv}
\]
is uniquely determined by the ordered pair
\[
\left(\widehat{\mathcal C}(D\mid Z,\pi),\Pi_D(Z,\pi)\right).
\]

Equivalently, if
\[
\widehat{\mathcal C}(D\mid Z,\pi)=\widehat{\mathcal C}(D\mid Z',\pi')
\quad\text{and}\quad
\Pi_D(Z,\pi)=\Pi_D(Z',\pi'),
\]
then
\[
(Z,\pi)\equiv(Z',\pi').
\]

## Consequence

The quotient
\[
\mathcal M_{\mathrm{aug}}^{(k)}(D)/{\equiv}
\]
injects into
\[
\mathfrak C_D^{(k)}\times\mathfrak P_D^{(k)}.
\]

Hence finiteness of the quotient follows once `\mathfrak C_D^{(k)}` and `\mathfrak P_D^{(k)}` are finite.
