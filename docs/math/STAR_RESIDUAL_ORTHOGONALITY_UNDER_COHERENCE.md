# Star Residual Orthogonality under Coherence

Conditional.

\[
\boxed{
\textbf{Star Residual Orthogonality under Coherence}
}
\]

\[
A=\{\mathrm{core},\mathrm{shell},\mathrm{transport},\mathrm{composition}\}.
\]

\[
D_\star-D_\star^\ast=\sum_{\alpha\in A}U_\alpha,
\qquad
\Phi_\alpha=\frac12\|U_\alpha\|^2.
\]

\[
|\langle U_\alpha,U_\gamma\rangle|
\le
\varepsilon_{\alpha\gamma}\|U_\alpha\|\,\|U_\gamma\|
\qquad
(\alpha\neq\gamma).
\]

\[
E_{\mathrm{phys}}=(\varepsilon_{\alpha\gamma})_{\alpha,\gamma\in A},
\qquad
\varepsilon_{\alpha\alpha}=0.
\]

\[
\rho(E_{\mathrm{phys}})<\frac12
\Rightarrow
\left|
\sum_{\alpha\neq\gamma}\langle U_\alpha,U_\gamma\rangle
\right|
\le
\theta\sum_\alpha \Phi_\alpha,
\qquad
\theta=2\rho(E_{\mathrm{phys}})<1.
\]
