# DraG0n Transport Under Diagnostic Equivalence Lemma

## Status

CONDITIONAL

## Statement

Fix an augmentation budget `k`.

Assume diagnostic equivalence `\sim` preserves:
1. admissible primitive wall alphabets,
2. admissible augmentation budgets,
3. induced frontier partition data,
4. score-component evaluation rules.

Then for every pair of diagnostically equivalent objects `D\sim D'`,
\[
\mathcal M_{\mathrm{aug}}^{(k)}(D)/{\equiv}
\cong
\mathcal M_{\mathrm{aug}}^{(k)}(D')/{\equiv}.
\]

## Transport form

There exists a quotient transport map
\[
T_{D,D'}^{(k)}:
\mathcal M_{\mathrm{aug}}^{(k)}(D)/{\equiv}
\to
\mathcal M_{\mathrm{aug}}^{(k)}(D')/{\equiv}
\]
which is bijective and preserves:
- admissibility,
- the preorder `\preceq`,
- the score value `\widehat{\mathcal C}`,
- the induced partition datum `\Pi_D`.

## Consequence

This isolates the remaining invariance-transport obstruction in the finite-quotient route.
