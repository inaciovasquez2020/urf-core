# Measure-Cycle Rigidity Target

Status: OPEN.

\[
\boxed{
\begin{minipage}{0.93\linewidth}
\textbf{Measure-Cycle Coercivity Theorem.}
Fix \(k,\Delta,R\). There exist constants
\[
\eta=\eta(k,\Delta,R)>0,\qquad C=C(k,\Delta,R)<\infty
\]
such that for every finite connected graph \(G\) of maximum degree \(\le\Delta\), every probability measure
\[
\mu\in \mathcal P(Q_R(G))
\]
on the quotient cycle space
\[
Q_R(G):=Z_1(G)/Z_1^{\le 2R+1}(G),
\]
and every finite-type observable family extracted from rooted radius-\(R\) neighborhoods, the following holds.

If
\[
\dim_{\mathbb F_2} Q_R(G)\ge C,
\]
then there exists a normalized observable
\[
\Phi_{G,R,\mu}
\]
with zero mean such that its cycle-average energy satisfies
\[
\mathcal E_{G,R,\mu}(\Phi_{G,R,\mu})\ge \eta.
\]

Equivalently, sufficiently large quotient cycle-rank forces a uniform positive coercive lower bound on the finite-type measure-cycle energy.
\end{minipage}
}
\]
