# Measure-Cycle Rigidity Target

Status: OPEN.

\[
\boxed{
\begin{minipage}{0.93\linewidth}
\textbf{Measure-Cycle Coercivity Theorem.}

Let
\[
\mathcal T_{k,\Delta,R}=\{\,\tau_1,\dots,\tau_N\,\}
\]
be the finite set of rooted radius-\(R\) \(k\)-types of connected graphs of maximum degree \(\le \Delta\). For each finite connected graph \(G\) of maximum degree \(\le\Delta\), let
\[
Q_R(G):=Z_1(G)/Z_1^{\le 2R+1}(G)
\]
be the quotient cycle space, and let
\[
\mu\in \mathcal P(Q_R(G))
\]
be a probability measure on \(Q_R(G)\).

Let
\[
\Phi_{G,R,\mu}\in \mathbb R^N
\]
be a finite-type observable extracted canonically from the rooted radius-\(R\) transport data and the cycle-measure \(\mu\), normalized by
\[
\langle \Phi_{G,R,\mu},\mathbf 1\rangle = 0,
\qquad
\|\Phi_{G,R,\mu}\|=1.
\]

Let
\[
\mathcal E_{G,R,\mu}(\Phi)
\]
denote the associated finite-type measure-cycle energy functional.

Then there exist constants
\[
\eta=\eta(k,\Delta,R)>0,\qquad C=C(k,\Delta,R)<\infty
\]
such that for every finite connected \(G\) with
\[
\dim_{\mathbb F_2}Q_R(G)\ge C,
\]
there exists such a normalized observable \(\Phi_{G,R,\mu}\) satisfying
\[
\mathcal E_{G,R,\mu}(\Phi_{G,R,\mu})\ge \eta.
\]

Equivalently, sufficiently large quotient cycle-rank forces a uniform positive coercive lower bound on finite-type measure-cycle energy.

Abbreviation:
\[
\mathrm{MCR}(k,\Delta,R).
\]

MCR(k,\Delta,R)

eta := inf \mathcal E_{G,R,\mu}(\Phi)
\end{minipage}
}
\]
