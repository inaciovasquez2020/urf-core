# DraG0n Finite Wall Alphabet Lemma

## Status

CONDITIONAL

## Statement

Fix a diagnostic object `D`.

Assume there exists a finite primitive wall alphabet
\[
\Omega_D
\]
such that every admissible augmentation is assembled from elements of `\Omega_D`.

Then the set of primitive wall-types available to admissible augmentations of `D` is finite.

## Budget corollary

If admissible augmentations in
\[
\mathcal M_{\mathrm{aug}}^{(k)}(D)
\]
use at most `k` primitive wall-types, then only finitely many primitive wall selections can occur.

## Consequence

This isolates A1 and A2 of `docs/math/DRAGON_EXPLICIT_ADMISSIBILITY_AXIOMS.md` as a standalone theorem object.
