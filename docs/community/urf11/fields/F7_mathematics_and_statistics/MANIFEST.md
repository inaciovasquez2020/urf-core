# F7_mathematics_and_statistics

## Status
OPEN

## Anchor problem
Prove a universal transfer theorem for multiscale inference and control: identify minimal conditions under which local certificates, sparse observations, and structural invariants lift to global predictive guarantees.

## Subproblems
- identifiability under structured missingness
- stability inequalities for regime transfer
- certainty-preserving compression of high-dimensional evidence
