# F1_agricultural_sciences_and_natural_resources

## Status
OPEN

## Anchor problem
Construct a climate-resilient agro-ecological control theory that jointly optimizes yield, soil stability, water balance, pathogen suppression, and biodiversity under multi-season uncertainty.

## Subproblems
- sparse nutrient-soil-crop state estimation
- early-warning dynamics for pests and crop disease
- adoption-stable intervention design under economic constraints

## Bridge targets
- biological_and_biomedical_sciences
- engineering
- geosciences_atmospheric_and_ocean_sciences
- health_sciences
- social_sciences

## Acceptance test
Improve one certified agro-ecological metric without degrading the others beyond a declared tolerance.
