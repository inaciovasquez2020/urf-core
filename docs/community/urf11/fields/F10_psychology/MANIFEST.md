# F10_psychology

## Status
OPEN

## Anchor problem
Construct a mechanistic cognitive-state theory that predicts behavior, learning, and intervention response across individuals, tasks, and contexts.

## Subproblems
- latent-state recovery from multimodal cognitive data
- causal modeling of learning and behavioral adaptation
- intervention design robust to heterogeneity and drift

## Bridge targets
- computer_and_information_sciences
- health_sciences
- mathematics_and_statistics
- multidisciplinary_interdisciplinary_sciences
- social_sciences

## Acceptance test
Improve out-of-task or out-of-population predictive validity under a declared protocol.
