# F10 epistemic resilience — exploratory research checkpoint

Status: OPEN. Scope: mathematical models of factual belief, personal meaning, social influence, and coping. Not a clinical instrument or validated theory. Existing F10 acceptance criteria remain unchanged: improve out-of-task or out-of-population prediction under a declared protocol.

This packet consolidates the research sequence through 2026-11-24. The proposed Meaning–Evidence Decoupling (MED) hypothesis is that preserving personal meaning independently of factual certainty may protect evidence responsiveness under social pressure. It competes with intellectual humility, self-affirmation, identity threat, and belief-persistence accounts. Neither novelty nor causal validity is established.

See MATHEMATICAL_RESULTS, IDENTIFIABILITY, EMPIRICAL_RESULTS, NOVELTY_AUDIT, FALSIFICATION_PROTOCOL and VERIFICATION_LEDGER. No human-subject records are included.
