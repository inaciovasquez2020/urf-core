# F2_biological_and_biomedical_sciences

## Status
OPEN

## Anchor problem
Build a mechanistic multiscale causal atlas linking genome, cell state, tissue dynamics, and organism-level phenotype with intervention-level predictivity.

## Subproblems
- causal reconstruction of cell-state transitions
- transport and signaling inference across scales
- intervention validation on out-of-domain perturbations

## Bridge targets
- agricultural_sciences_and_natural_resources
- computer_and_information_sciences
- health_sciences
- mathematics_and_statistics
- physical_sciences

## Acceptance test
Reduce phenotype prediction error or intervention uncertainty on a held-out biological regime.
