## Anchor problem
Solve the adaptive policy-design problem for strategic societies under incomplete information, endogenous networks, and feedback-driven behavior change.

## Subproblems
- policy inference under endogenous response and partial observability
- network contagion and coordination dynamics
- mechanism design for adoption, trust, and compliance
