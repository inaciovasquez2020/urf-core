# F4_engineering

## Status
OPEN

## Anchor problem
Construct self-healing, provably safe, adaptive infrastructure that remains operational under cascading failures, adversarial disturbances, and uncertain demand.

## Subproblems
- fault localization on coupled physical networks
- adaptive control with formal safety envelopes
- resilience metrics for repair and reconfiguration

## Bridge targets
- agricultural_sciences_and_natural_resources
- computer_and_information_sciences
- geosciences_atmospheric_and_ocean_sciences
- health_sciences
- physical_sciences
- social_sciences

## Acceptance test
Produce a certified gain in resilience or safety under a declared disturbance class.
