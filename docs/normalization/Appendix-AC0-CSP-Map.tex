\section{Scope of CSP Examples}
Bounded constraint arity does not imply bounded incidence degree:
general 2-SAT and Horn-SAT can have unbounded-degree variables.
Fixed-template, bounded-width and restricted propagation algorithms
must be checked against explicit access, degree, wiring, initialization,
and output hypotheses. The existence of local propagation rules does
not establish bounded aggregate information per round.
No unconditional solver-class separation or exclusion of an entire
CSP family follows from these examples.
