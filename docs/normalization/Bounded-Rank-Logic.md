# Bounded quantifier rank versus finite-variable logic

**Status: automatic replacement withdrawn; semantic bridge OPEN.** See [AC0 mathematical erratum](../math/AC0_CSP_NORMALIZATION_ERRATUM_2026_11_26.md). Historical statements remain available at the source blobs listed there.

Bounded quantifier rank and a fixed bound on the number of variables are different restrictions. A rank-one formula (x=x) AND (exists y P(y)) can depend on a predicate arbitrarily far from x, even on a connected path. Hence arbitrary bounded-rank formulas are not determined by a fixed rooted neighborhood. A ball-restricted language or a justified local fragment is needed. Do not substitute bounded-rank logic for FO^k in earlier results without proving the translation and checking all hypotheses and costs. Previously verified results retain their exact statements.
