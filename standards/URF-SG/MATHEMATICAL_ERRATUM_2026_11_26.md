# URF-SG mathematical erratum

Source: [Spectral Gap Rigidity Wall](URF_Spectral_Gap_Rigidity_Wall.tex), blob 5dc4ed09d925cceccb78b72653781cfce52a6872. Paper-level corrections; no new Lean certificate.

For an orthogonal projector P onto excluded symmetry modes, H0=ker(P)=(ran P)^perp, not (ker P)^perp=ran P. Assume the reduced form domain contains a nonzero vector. For a nonnegative self-adjoint operator L, positivity of the Rayleigh infimum on D(L) intersect H0 is equivalent to coercivity there and to absence of unit vectors with energies tending to zero. This elementary equivalence remains valid after correcting the space. If a self-adjoint operator restriction is intended, H0 must reduce L; alternatively specify a densely defined closed restricted quadratic form. The energy floor c applies to unit vectors, or scales as c times squared norm, not a uniform cost for arbitrarily small nonzero amplitudes.

Ornstein-Uhlenbeck: the literal erroneous reduced space consists of constants, and L1=0, so its Rayleigh infimum is zero. On the corrected mean-zero space the standard Gaussian Hermite basis has eigenvalues j=0,1,2,...; removing the constant mode gives exact gap 1, attained by x. Thus the corrected external example is classical and valid.

Heisenberg: the literal erroneous reduced space retains the zero vertical Fourier mode, including the constant function, hence the gap there is zero. On the corrected space with nonzero vertical frequencies, specify a compatible compact lattice, descending horizontal vector fields, Haar measure and central period ell. With [X,Y]=partial_z and skew-adjoint horizontal fields, on a mode satisfying partial_z u=i omega u the squared norm of (X plus or minus iY)u implies ||Xu||^2+||Yu||^2 >= |omega| ||u||^2. For periodic central coordinate of period ell the allowed frequencies are 2 pi k/ell, so the nonzero-mode gap is at least 2 pi/ell (by orthogonal mode decomposition, extended on the form domain). The original 4 pi value is not justified without the period or another appropriate normalization. This is a conditional bound on the specified quotient, not a universal lattice-independent constant.

The two literal external certificates therefore fail; their corrected versions have the stated separate hypotheses. Finite Ritz values alone are not certified lower bounds on the full Rayleigh infimum. Historical PDF and source blobs preserved.
